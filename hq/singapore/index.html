The playbook.

The 5 questions: ask, don't pitch
  1. "What's the one thing you wish people understood about your brand?"Shows how they see their brand and where it falls short.
  2. "Who handles your creative today, in-house or an agency?"Shows whether there's room for us, and who decides.
  3. "What's coming up in the next 6 months: launches, events, a new space?"Gives us a reason and a deadline to reach out later.
  4. "Where do your best clients or members find you?"Tells us which channel to fix first.
  5. "If you could fix one thing about how you show up online, what would it be?"This becomes the spec sample. Write it down word for word.

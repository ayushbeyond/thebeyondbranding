How you earn

Four separate income streams. Keep the setup fee and commission separate: commission is your upside, not a discount on the work.

One-off

Phase 1 fee

[£2,450] suggested for 5 ranges. 50% at start, 50% at go-live.

Monthly · % of sales

Amazon commission

Lower rate on all Amazon sales: you built the channel. Tracked with Amazon's free tracking links.

Per deal · higher %

Bulk-deal commission

Leads you register are yours for 90 days, whichever way they buy.

Monthly · recurring

Screen content

Every screen sold needs menus, promos and window loops. Sell a monthly content pack to the buyer.

Amazon in 12 words

The only terms you need to follow the conversation with Bal or the e-commerce partner.

Seller CentralBal's seller account. He owns it and adds you as a user.
ASINAmazon's ID for one product page. Each size gets one.
Parent / childOne listing per range, sizes as options. Reviews pool together.
GTIN / GS1The barcode each size needs. Bought from GS1 UK.
Brand RegistryProves AIOLEC owns the brand. Needs a UK trademark.
A+ contentThe rich image section lower on the page.
Brand StoreAIOLEC's mini website inside Amazon.
FBA / FBMAmazon ships it / AIOLEC ships it. Start with FBM for big screens.
Referral feeAmazon's cut per sale. Electronics is 7% in the UK.
Amazon BusinessThe B2B side: VAT-free prices, bulk tiers, quote requests.
AttributionFree tracking links. Your proof for commission.
Sponsored ProductsPay-per-click ads in Amazon search results.

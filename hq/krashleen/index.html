    Rules from the chat

    Guardrails.

    ◎
    Never change a client's brand identityTheir fonts, colours and logo are the base for everything. Rule number 1.
    ≡
    Keep proposals simpleDeliverables, price and a few slides about you. Extra copy belongs in brand decks, not proposals.
    ↑
    Look busy, not availableNo "open to work" or "open to remote". Show the clients you already have.
    !
    Don't spam LinkedIn10–20 personal messages a day. Blasts get the account restricted.
    ÷
    Write the split before the workOne line per deal: who leads, your commission or %, who invoices. Two minutes now saves the awkward talk later.
    ♡
    Only real storiesThe handmade, honest persona is the edge. Keep content true: one staged post that gets exposed undoes it.

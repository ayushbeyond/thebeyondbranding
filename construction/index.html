Questions directors ask

We get our work from referrals. Why do we need this?

Referrals still look you up before they call. A strong site turns a name someone mentioned into a firm they feel safe hiring, and it helps you win tenders against bigger names.

We are busy on site. How much of our time does it take?

About 30 minutes: one call and a folder of project photos. We write the copy and build everything else.

Can our current web person build it?

Yes. We can hand over the design for them to build, or build it ourselves and give them the keys.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain stays in your name, and you get the files and logins at handover.

Do you work outside London?

Yes. We work with firms across the UK, Ireland and the EU, with studio presence in London, Dubai and Mumbai.

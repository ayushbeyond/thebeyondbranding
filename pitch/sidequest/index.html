07 / FAQ

Side
notes

Is it a gig or a party?

Both, kind of. Live rappers and live DJs share one small room, so it's closer than a concert and more about the music than a club night.

Can I come alone?

Yes, and plenty will. The night is built for meeting people who are into the same sound. Bring your crew if you'd rather.

Why only 50?

So you can actually move, hear the system properly and get to the bar without a fight. Once fifty passes are gone, the room is full.

Where do I get tickets?

On Sort My Scene. The first 20 are ₹150 early bird.

Where is it?

Indore. The venue is announced soon. Follow along so you don't miss it.

The idea

Vagabode is a small travelling museum you can wear. Every shirt carries a painting, a film or a faraway place, and a note on how to wear it. Wander the rooms, pick a piece, and send us a message. That’s the whole checkout.

Wander

Four rooms instead of categories. The compass follows you around and points to the next piece.

Look closer

Each shirt has a wall label: the artist, the story, the palette, and a stylist’s note for the full look.

Wear the story

No cart, no checkout. Request any piece on WhatsApp, or fill your satchel and send them all at once.

The collection

Twenty shirts, four rooms, one compass.

The collection is hung like an exhibition: the Surrealists, the Masters and Moderns, a dark Cinema Hall, and the Riders & Roads. Tap any piece to read its wall label.

The specimen plates

Every shirt, drawn by hand and set in motion.

Photographs sell the fit. Illustrations sell the story. Each piece gets its own plate, drawn the way old naturalists drew their finds: an inked outline first, then the colour flooding in. Then the print comes alive. On this one the clouds drift, the flag flies and the wildflowers sway.

Drawn from the real shirt: cobalt sky, sculpted cream clouds, the manor and its tower, and wildflowers along the hem.

  • Plates double as hang tags, stickers and story covers
  • One per shirt, so the grid has a signature look
  • Animated versions loop on Reels and in WhatsApp status

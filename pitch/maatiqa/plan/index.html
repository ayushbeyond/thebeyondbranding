Shopify, or a site of your own?

You're starting slowly: a first collection, made-to-order pieces, commissions and a handful of orders a month. This page sets out what Shopify actually costs in India, what you'd genuinely miss without it, and what a custom site plus WhatsApp would cost to run, so you can decide on facts rather than fear of missing out.

The short answer. At 5–15 orders a month, most of what Shopify sells you is software you won't use yet, and in India it also takes an extra 2% of every online sale. A custom site with Razorpay payment links, WhatsApp and a shipping aggregator covers a slow launch. Shopify starts to pay for itself once you're stocking ready-made pieces and processing orders every day. The thresholds are set out below.

01 · The honest list

What you'd actually miss without Shopify.

Shopify is excellent software. The question is which of its features matter for a studio that sells a few pieces a month, many of them made to order. Here's every feature people usually worry about, rated for MAATIQA today.

Shopify featureNeeded now?What covers it on a custom site
Cart and one-click checkoutLaterA Razorpay Payment Page or Button per piece takes UPI, cards and netbanking with no monthly fee.4 Multi-item carts matter more once people buy sets.
Editing products yourselfYesThis is the real gap. Fix it by moving the collection into one simple editable list (a Google Sheet or a small JSON file) so adding a piece doesn't need a developer.
Inventory and stock countsNot yetMostly made to order with each piece slightly different, so a "sold" or "made to order" label is enough.
Order dashboard and emailsLaterThe Razorpay dashboard lists every payment and sends receipts. A WhatsApp chat label per order ("paid", "printing", "shipped") works at this volume.
Shipping labels and trackingYesShiprocket works without Shopify. Its free Lite plan averages about ₹45 a shipment for up to 5 orders a month.9
GST invoicesYesRazorpay Invoices issues GST-compliant bills.4 Registration is needed either way once you ship out of state (see the checklist).
Discount codes, abandoned-cart emailsNot yetThese are growth tools for paid-ads traffic. Your buyers arrive through Instagram and conversation, so a personal WhatsApp follow-up converts better.
Customer accounts, reviews appsNot yetHand-picked testimonials and real photos from buyers on the site do the trust job better at this stage.
Instagram / Google product catalogue syncLaterMeta Commerce Manager accepts a manual catalogue, and the WhatsApp Business app has its own catalogue and cart.8
24/7 support, hosting, securityCoveredThe site is static (no database or login), so there is little to break or hack. Payments are handled entirely on Razorpay's side.

What you gain by staying custom: a site that looks like MAATIQA rather than a theme, no monthly bill, no 2% surcharge, full ownership of the code and domain, and an enquiry-first flow that suits commissions, hospitality projects and made-to-order pieces, which a cart doesn't sell well.

03 · The custom route, phased

Start lean. Add only what the orders ask for.

Each phase reuses the same design, so nothing gets thrown away. If MAATIQA outgrows it, the brand system moves onto a Shopify theme intact.

Phase 1 · LaunchNow

Brand site + conversation

  • The concept site, made official: real names, prices, sizes and photos
  • "Enquire" opens WhatsApp with the piece name pre-filled, instead of copying text into Instagram
  • Razorpay Payment Link sent in chat once size and finish are agreed
  • Shiprocket Lite for labels and tracking
  • WhatsApp Business app: catalogue, quick replies, order labels
Running cost: domain ~₹1,000/yr + 2.36% per online payment
Phase 2 · Steady10–25 orders/mo

Buy buttons + automation

  • Razorpay Payment Button on ready-to-ship pieces, so buyers can pay without chatting
  • Collection driven by an editable sheet she controls
  • WhatsApp Cloud API through an official provider: automatic order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Workshop booking page with a fixed price
Adds ~₹0–1,500/mo (WhatsApp provider) + paise per message
Phase 3 · SwitchTriggers below

Move the shop to Shopify

  • 30+ orders a month, or orders arriving daily
  • 20+ ready-made pieces held in stock with variants
  • Paid ads becoming a main channel (they need carts, discount codes and recovery emails)
  • Someone other than the founder handling orders
At that point the surcharge per order is small and the software earns its keep

06 · Brand, sharpened

Make the layer line something people keep.

The identity already has its big idea: every surface is a stack of lines of earth. These ideas turn it from a look into things a buyer holds, shares and remembers. None of them need Shopify; most work better on a site you control.

Signature idea

The Layer Passport

Every piece ships with a small card stating its own numbers: layer count, print hours, clay, firing date. A QR code links to a page on maatiqa.com with the short film of that exact piece being printed. It costs almost nothing, proves "it's clay, not plastic", and gives every buyer a reason to post.

02

Numbered editions

Release forms in small numbered runs (say 12 of each). Scarcity is honest here because printing and firing are slow, and it justifies a price above mass "terracotta décor".

03

Failure as a format

Your audience shows up for what goes wrong: collapses, cracks, the hammer. Give those reels a recurring name and slot. Collapsed prints can become a tiny "second life" line of shards, coasters or planters.

04

Hindi names, kept

Jaal, Bhanwar, Kumud: each form gets a word and its meaning. Approve the final list and use it everywhere (labels, captions, invoices) so the names become the product.

05

The green seal

The layered M as a stamped seal on kraft packaging, layer-striped tissue and a forest-green thread. Unboxing is free reach on Instagram.

06

Made for this space

A commissions series: hotel table, café counter, architect's lobby. Each one documented as a mini case study. These bigger-ticket projects sell through enquiry, not a cart.

07

Studio hours

The open-workshop programme as a second revenue line and a community engine: fixed monthly dates, a booking link, workshop photos reposted with credit.

Keep: forest green #3E4B3D as home, Newsreader and Instrument Sans, a calm and specific voice. Avoid: AI-generated product photos presented as real stock, "luxury artisanal elegance" phrasing, and claims the studio can't stand behind (the copy pass already removed "5,000 years" and "returns to the ground").

07 · Instagram, from your own numbers

The reach is there. The follow isn't, yet.

@maatiqa_studio has 12 posts and 160 followers, yet nine visible reels have drawn about 41,000 views between them (≈4,600 each). Instagram is already showing MAATIQA to strangers; the job is turning views into follows and enquiries.

Stories beat showcases.

The hammer, the making process and the founder detailing a vase are the top three. The product-only "Vases that feel like art" is last, at under a quarter of the hammer reel's views.

The hook carries it.

"[THE] Hammer" (8,481) opens on a question: why is she holding a mallet? "Nooo no no" (2,087), a failed print, shows the mess straight away. Open on the question, not the answer.

Built for sends.

Instagram weights watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach most, and DM sends count 3–5× more than likes for reaching non-followers.10 "Is it clay or plastic?" and failed-print reels are exactly the kind people forward.

1,000 followers unlocks Trial Reels.

Trial Reels show a reel to non-followers first so you can test hooks safely; they're open to public accounts with 1,000+ followers.10 Make that the first milestone.

08 · True either way

Things that don't change with the platform.

GST before shipping out of state

Selling from your own site (Shopify or custom) and shipping to another state generally requires GST registration before the first inter-state sale; the small-seller exemption covers marketplace sales only.11 Confirm with your CA, as this decides your pricing.

Fragile shipping is the hidden cost

Clay breaks. Budget double-wall boxes, honeycomb wrap and a breakage policy (photo on arrival, replace or refund). Price shipping by weight slab; a 1.5 kg parcel costs far more than the "from ₹20" headline rate.9

Real photography

Before any launch, replace concept and AI campaign images of products with real photos of real pieces. Trust is the brand.

The market is there

India's home décor market was valued at about US$26.9 billion in 2025 and is forecast to grow about 5% a year to 2034, with online and sustainable products among the drivers.12 The slow launch is a choice, not a ceiling.

Stay custom if

You're selling by conversation.

  • Under ~25 orders a month
  • Most pieces made to order or one-off
  • Commissions and hospitality matter as much as retail
  • You want the brand to look like nobody else
Move to Shopify when

You're selling by volume.

  • 30+ orders a month, or orders every day
  • A stocked catalogue of 20+ pieces with variants
  • Paid ads are a main channel
  • Someone else processes orders without the founder

Shopify, simply · for MAATIQA · November 2026

Don't pay for a shop yet. Pay to be seen.

MAATIQA's problem today isn't checkout. People are watching the reels but not yet following or buying. In its first year, Shopify would cost roughly what a brand film, two dozen edited reels and a real product shoot cost together. Here's the short version, with the receipts.

01 · First, the name

One name everywhere: MAATIQA.

"Studio" describes what MAATIQA is; it isn't part of the name. The exact-name .com is available, which is rare and worth taking immediately. The goal is one word people can remember and type: maatiqa.com, @maatiqa, hello@maatiqa.com.

Website domain

maatiqa.comChoose thisShortest, the brand name exactly, and .com is the global default that signals no single country1, which matters for hotels, architects and buyers outside India later.
maatiqastudio.comSkipLonger, easy to mistype, and it ties the brand to a descriptor.
maatiqa.inOptional extra.in signals an Indian brand to local buyers1. Buy it cheaply as a redirect to protect the name, not as the main address.

Instagram username

@maatiqaIf availableMatches the domain exactly. Display name: "MAATIQA · Ceramic Design Studio", so search for "ceramic" still finds it.
@maatiqa_studioFallbackKeep it if @maatiqa is taken. It's clear; it just doesn't match the domain.
  • Followers, posts and the account itself stay when the username changes.2
  • Old links to @maatiqa_studio stop working, so update the bio link, website and any print.2
  • Instagram holds the old name for 14 days so you can switch back; after that anyone can take it.2 Claim @maatiqa_studio on a second account pointing to @maatiqa.
  • At 160 followers, now is the cheapest moment to switch.

04 · When Shopify becomes the right call

Shopify's fixed cost shrinks as orders grow.

Shopify's monthly fee is the same at 5 orders or 80. Spread across more orders, the cost per order drops until it's a small price for the time the software saves.

Shopify cost per ₹3,000 order (plan + apps + 2% cut)

Basic plan billed yearly with GST, ~₹1,000 a month of apps, and Shopify's 2% cut with GST. Setup cost not included.

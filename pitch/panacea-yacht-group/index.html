What we’d build

Six deliverables. Each one fixes a real gap.

01 · Social

2027 lineup carousel

The problem: Stock-looking boat ads blend in, and buyers spot a wrong hull in a second.

  • 10 slides, one per series, on Zodiac’s own photos
  • Slides 1 and 2 form one panorama
  • Dealer lockup and a sea-trial call to action
  • Story versions and a template for new boats

One post that works as an ad, a story and a sales handout.

02 · Website

Zodiac series hub

The problem: Only XCC gets a full story, and there’s no Zodiac path to a sea trial.

  • A page per series: photos, specs, who it suits
  • “Find your Zodiac”: five questions, one series
  • A live in-stock strip from your listings
  • Sea-trial booking for Brielle or Fort Lauderdale

From browsing to a booked sea trial in one visit.

03 · Inventory

Listing & inventory kit

The problem: Your boats appear on YachtWay, boats.com and broker sites, each with its own photo order and headline style.

  • A shot list and photo order for every boat
  • A branded cover image with the dealer lockup
  • One copy structure: headline, highlights, specs, CTA
  • A one-page spec sheet for walk-ins and email

Every listing, on every site, looks like one premium dealer.

04 · Ads

Sea-trial lead engine

The problem: Buyers on the Jersey Shore and in South Florida don’t yet know Panacea sells Zodiac.

  • Instagram and Facebook ads near both offices
  • An instant form: series, timing, preferred office
  • Reply scripts for call, text and email
  • A monthly report: leads, trials, cost per trial

A cost per booked sea trial you can see every month.

05 · Social

Monthly content system

The problem: Zodiac posts compete with your big-yacht listings, and posting depends on who has time.

  • Four pillars: boats, life on the water, advice, the dealer
  • 12 posts and 4 reels a month from shared templates
  • A phone shot list for trials and deliveries
  • Owner stories, shared with permission

A feed that keeps selling between boat shows.

06 · In person

Boat show & delivery kit

The problem: Shows and handovers are where buyers meet you, and where leads get lost on paper.

  • Booth graphics and series signs in the same look
  • QR cards that open the sea-trial form
  • A follow-up email sequence for show leads
  • A welcome pack and photo moment for new owners

Every handshake reaches your lead list.

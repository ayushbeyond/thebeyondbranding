How we work

From drawing to dispatch, we handle it

Six steps, the same for every order. You deal with one contact from the first message to delivery.

Processing
Cutting, edging and drilling to drawing before toughening or lamination.
  1. 01
    Share your requirementDrawings, a BOQ, a window schedule or a photo on WhatsApp. Whatever you have.
  2. 02
    Technical reviewWe check glass type, thickness, processing and fittings against the application.
  3. 03
    Quotation and samplesA line-item quote with lead times. Samples where available.
  4. 04
    Order and scheduleConfirmed sizes and quantities, with delivery split by floor or phase if needed.
  5. 05
    Processing and checksCut, edged, drilled, toughened, laminated or assembled, then inspected before packing.
  6. 06
    Packing, dispatch and supportLabelled, protected packing and delivery coordinated with your site team.

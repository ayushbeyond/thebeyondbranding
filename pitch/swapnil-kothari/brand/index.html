06 · Rules

Guardrails that keep it credible.

Real photography onlyOriginal stage, campus and portrait photographs. Never generated or altered faces.
One official fact sheetStudent numbers, titles and institution roles approved by his office before any number is published.
Exact award wording“Bharat Gaurav Award 2023, presented at the House of Commons, London.”
Personal, not partisanNo party marks in the identity. Public life lives on personal channels, separate from the institutions.
Institutions keep their identitiesOfficial logos and links only, never recreated or merged into this mark.
Restrained motionSlow reveals and image fades. No particles, spinning globes or counters.

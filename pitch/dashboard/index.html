How to pitch one lead (2 minutes each)
  1. Look first. Tap Preview and check the page reads right for them. Open their Instagram and look at their last few posts.
  2. Grab the screenshot. Tap Screenshot to save it. Send it with the link, since some people won't click a link from a stranger.
  3. Instagram: tap Copy DM, then Instagram. Paste the message in their DMs and attach the screenshot.
  4. WhatsApp: tap WhatsApp. The message is already typed. Attach the screenshot and send.
  5. Email: tap Gmail. The email opens in your account, already written. Attach the screenshot and send. (Drafts with the screenshot are already in your Gmail for the leads with an email.)
  6. Log it. Set the status to Sent. A follow-up date 3 days out is filled in for you.
  7. Follow up on the date with a short "Did you get a chance to look?" When someone taps I'm interested on their page, you get a WhatsApp with their ref code. Set them to Interested.

Order: start with leads marked "Website gap", then "Upgrade". Do the "Audit first" ones last, after checking their current site.

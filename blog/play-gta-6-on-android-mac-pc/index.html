GTA 6 on Android, Mac and PC.

What actually works today, what doesn't, and what to ignore.

By AyushUpdated 27 Nov 2026

GTA 6 came out on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no Android, Mac or PC version. You can still play it on those devices if you own it on a console, by streaming it with Remote Play.

Quick answerAndroid, Mac and Windows PC can all play GTA 6 through Remote Play from your own PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. Xbox Cloud Gaming does not stream it to these devices, and there is no native PC version yet.

Android

Install PS Remote Play (Android 9 or later) or the Xbox app from the Play Store, sign in with your console's account and connect. A Bluetooth DualSense or Xbox controller works, and clip-on controllers make the phone feel like a handheld. The setup steps are the same as in our iPhone and iPad guide.

Mac

Sony's PS Remote Play app runs on Mac. Xbox owners can stream from their console in a browser at xbox.com/play by choosing their console. Connect the controller over Bluetooth or USB. There is no macOS version of GTA 6, and none has been announced.

Windows PC

PC players can use PS Remote Play or the Xbox app to stream from a console. Microsoft has said Xbox Cloud Gaming on PC does not include GTA 6, so don't buy a cloud subscription for that. A native PC release hasn't been announced; see our GTA 6 PC release date page for the latest.

What about cloud gaming services?

At launch, GTA 6 isn't available on GeForce NOW, Boosteroid, Amazon Luna or Shadow, and PlayStation's server-based cloud streaming has no confirmed GTA 6 listing. Xbox Cloud Gaming covers Xbox consoles only. If a service claims otherwise, check its official game list first.

Avoid scams: "GTA 6 APK", "GTA 6 PC download" and "GTA 6 for Mac" files are all fake. Don't install them.

FAQ

Is there a GTA 6 APK for Android?

No. Any GTA 6 APK is fake. The game is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC?

Not natively yet. You can stream it to a PC with Remote Play from a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S you own.

Can I play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

Not as an installed game, but Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming.

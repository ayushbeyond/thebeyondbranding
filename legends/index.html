02 The mission

Built for the businesses we grew up with.

Some of India's most extraordinary businesses are hiding behind ordinary branding.

A neighbourhood shop. A street-side food stall. A family-run business. A local maker with a story worth telling. They have the craft, the regulars and the reputation. What they rarely have is an identity that matches.

Local Legends gives them one. Designed by the same studio that builds brands for clients in London, Dubai and Mumbai.

05 Nominate a Legend

Know a local legend?

Tell us about a business in your city that deserves a bigger identity. It takes two minutes.

Who can be nominated

Independent, owner-run local businesses in India: stalls, carts, shops, eateries, makers and family businesses. The owner must be happy to take part and be featured.

How we choose

  • A real story and genuine local love
  • Great product or service, held back by its branding
  • Where good design can make a visible difference

What's included

Brand identity, real-world applications, a digital launch and a feature as a numbered edition. All pro bono.

Please note: we select one business per edition, so a nomination does not guarantee selection. We'll only contact the business with the owner's consent.
